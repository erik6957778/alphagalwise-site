Privacy Policy

Applies to: the Alpha-Gal Wise™ app and alphagalwise.com, operated by WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGIES LLC.
Version: Rev F. Effective date: November 4, 2026.

The short version.

  • No account. Your triggers, settings, scans, label photos, and typed corrections stay on your device unless you choose to Share or Send them.
  • By default, nothing we receive identifies you.
  • When the app opens, it checks our server for settings; our hosting provider logs your IP address for security and delivery and keeps it up to 7 days. Nothing else is sent unless you choose: anonymous ingredient reports (off unless you turn them on), and label photos you Share or corrections you Send, one at a time.
  • We don't sell your data or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who we are

Alpha-Gal Wise™ is published by WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGIES LLC, an Oklahoma limited liability company (“we,” “us”). Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy adds details for Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut residents.

Information we collect

By default, nothing that identifies you. The app never asks for your name, email, or phone number.

Kept only on your device (we don't receive it): triggers and settings, recent scans, cached product data, consent choices, saved label photos, and unsent corrections.

Every time the app opens: the settings check and IP log described above.

Only if you choose: anonymous ingredient reports; label photos you Share and corrections you Send; and emails you send us (address, message, attachments; please leave out health details you'd rather not share).

Other companies receive data when you look up products, buy, or visit the website. See Third-party services.

Information we do not collect

Your name, account details, contacts, precise location, device or advertising identifiers, triggers or allergens, symptoms, or medical records. The website uses no cookies (see Website analytics), and the app has no analytics or crash-reporting tools that identify you.

How we use information

  • To run features you ask for, like barcode lookup.
  • To improve ingredient matching and fix wrong ratings or misread label text, using anonymous reports.
  • To review photos you Share and corrections you Send and, if they pass review, add them to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts.
  • To answer your emails.
  • To keep our systems secure and comply with the law.

No profiling, and no use of reports, photos, or corrections for advertising.

No advertising

The app shows no ads, contains no advertising software, and shares nothing with advertisers or ad networks.

Donations and in-app purchases

Optional tips are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We don't receive your name, Apple ID, Google account, or card—only a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the app can thank you.

Anonymous ingredient reports (opt-in)

Off unless you turn it on (when the app asks, or Settings → Help improve ingredient matching). Turn it off anytime; no feature depends on it. Reports cover ingredients the app couldn't recognize or rated Avoid or Ambiguous.

A report contains: the ingredient involved; how the app sorted other label text into notes, headings or section labels, strengths (e.g. “16%”), descriptors, and origin or raw-material notes (up to 40 pieces, 200 characters each); neighboring ingredient names for context (up to 400 characters), including for unrecognized ingredients; up to 40 flagged ingredients and the app's general rating for each; the barcode (if any); the public product name, brand, and full ingredient text; the label source (Open Food Facts or a sister project, openFDA, or your photo); app version; platform (iOS, Android, or web); date (UTC, no time of day); and a random ID new for each report and never reused. At most one report per product is sent from your phone.

Never contains: your name, email, or account; device, advertising, or installation identifiers; triggers, allergens, or other settings; location; cookies; or photos. We don't store your IP address with reports. The rating comes from the app's built-in dictionary, not your settings.

Text from photos: only the extracted ingredient text (including neighboring words) is sent, after automatic removal of things that look like phone numbers, email addresses, web links, or long numbers.

Use: only to improve ingredient matching and fix wrong ratings or misread text, including adding unrecognized ingredients. Never sold or used for ads. We may publish aggregated ingredient information with no individual reports; any derived from Open Food Facts data is under the Open Database License. Reports aren't sent to Open Food Facts or its sister projects automatically.

De-identified: reports are collected de-identified, we will keep them that way and not try to re-identify anyone, and any service provider handling them must commit to the same.

Region: hidden, and no reports sent, in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland (see Region handling).

Photo of ingredients

On your device only. If you choose Accept, the app saves the photo, the text read from it (read on-device), the barcode, and the date on your device (or browser storage on the web). We don't receive these. If you choose Not now, the photo is used once and discarded.

Sharing is per photo: a photo leaves your device only if you tap Share on it. Web photos and photos imported from your library can only be saved, not shared.

If you tap Share, we receive: the photo, cropped and re-saved without location or camera data; its text, scrubbed of things like phone numbers, emails, and long numbers; the barcode; the date (no time); app version and platform; the consent version; and a random withdrawal code. We do not receive your name, email, account, device or advertising identifiers, location, triggers or settings, or other photos.

Why: to review it and, if usable, add it and its ingredient data to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts (cosmetics and personal care), or Open Products Facts (household products), improving product data for everyone. No advertising, profiling, tracking, or sale, and no sharing except with those projects.

Review and publication. A person (the publisher) reviews every photo; photos showing people or personal information are deleted. Approved photos are submitted from a separate Alpha-Gal Wise app account and become public under CC BY-SA 3.0 or any later version adopted by that project (photos) and ODbL/DbCL (data). Once published, we cannot withdraw or delete them. Each submission carries one fixed app-wide ID, the same for all users, which cannot identify you or your phone. All three projects are run by the Open Food Facts association, an independent nonprofit in France with its own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices: delete saved photos (one or all) in Settings → Label photos; withdraw unpublished shared photos (we delete them from our servers); or turn off “Ask to share.” We can find shared photos only through the code on your device; if you deleted the app, email support@alphagalwise.com with the date and product and we'll try to find and delete them.

Health information. Using an alpha-gal app can suggest a health condition, so we collect shared photos only with your separate consent (Share), keep them de-identified, and don't try to re-identify anyone. See the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Please don't photograph children, or anyone else.

Product corrections

If you tap Send on a correction to a product's name, brand, or ingredient text, we receive: the corrected text; barcode; category you chose; any attached photo (cropped, without location or camera data); date (no time); app version and platform; consent version; and a random code to withdraw it before publication. Not your name, email, account, device or advertising identifiers, location, or triggers.

A person reviews every correction. Approved ones are submitted from a separate Alpha-Gal Wise app account to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts and become public and permanent (text under ODbL/DbCL, photos under CC BY-SA). Public edit history shows the app account, not you, and the same app-wide ID as photos. Only send text printed on the product, and nothing about yourself.

Retention and region rules for photos and corrections: Data retention, Region handling.

Third-party services and data sources

  • Open Food Facts (nonprofit, crowdsourced) and sister projects Open Beauty Facts and Open Products Facts: when you scan, the app looks up the barcode directly with Open Food Facts, which receives the barcode and your IP address like any website and may return a sister-project record. Shared photos and sent corrections go through our server, so Open Food Facts doesn't get your IP for those. Licenses: see credits. Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • openFDA (U.S. FDA public data, for some drug labels): receives the lookup and your IP address. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDA.
  • Supabase (with network provider Cloudflare), in the United States: hosts our database and storage, answers the launch settings check, receives reports, photos, and corrections for us, and logs IP addresses (up to 7 days).
  • Apple and Google: distribute the app and process purchases under their own policies (Apple, Google).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. No cookies, ads, or cross-site tracking.
  • GoatCounter: website visit counter only, never in the app (see below).
  • ImprovMX (forwarding) and Google Gmail (hosting): handle email to support@alphagalwise.com.

About the data. Product data is crowdsourced or from openFDA drug labels and may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Always check the label.

Website analytics

alphagalwise.com uses GoatCounter, a privacy-first analytics service, to count visits. It records the page visited, the referring site, browser and device type, screen size, and approximate country and region. It uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not store your IP address; the IP is used only briefly to estimate location and then discarded. We see only totals, cannot identify you, and never sell or share this data or use it for ads. We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. The Alpha-Gal Wise™ app itself has no analytics.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information or consumer health data, or share it for targeted advertising. We share only:

  • with service providers (Supabase, Cloudflare, ImprovMX, Google) under contracts limiting their use;
  • with Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts, only for photos and corrections you choose to send that pass review;
  • as aggregated ingredient information that identifies no one;
  • when the law requires, or to protect the rights or security of our users, us, or others;
  • in a merger, sale, or transfer of the business, where the new owner must follow this policy.

Data retention

  • On your device (triggers, settings, scans, saved photos): until you delete it or remove the app.
  • Anonymous ingredient reports: deleted or reduced to aggregate statistics within 90 days.
  • Shared photos and corrections: deleted right after human review, published or rejected; deleted automatically if not reviewed within 30 days of upload. (The 90-day period applies only to reports.)
  • Record of published items: only the project product code, image ID or fields changed, consent version, and date, to handle removal requests and show the license.
  • Hosting logs (Supabase / Cloudflare) with IP addresses: up to 7 days, never combined with reports, photos, or corrections.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you and keep basic records; you can ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Device data: delete in Settings or remove the app.
  • Ingredient reports: turn off Settings → Help improve ingredient matching; future reports stop immediately. Past reports carry no identifier, so we can't find one person's; they're deleted or aggregated within 90 days.
  • Photos and corrections: withdraw unpublished ones in Settings → Label photos or Settings → My corrections, or turn off “Ask to share.”
  • Emails: ask us to access or delete them.

Withdrawing doesn't undo anything already published; contact us and we'll forward a removal request to the project.

State privacy rights (including Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: confirm whether we collect, share, or process your personal or consumer health data and get a copy; get a list of third parties we share it with; correct or delete it; withdraw consent; and opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or profiling (we do none of these). Washington, Nevada, and Connecticut residents: see the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for health-data rights and appeals.

To make a request, email support@alphagalwise.com. We respond within 45 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights. We may be unable to link some data (like anonymous reports) to you; if so, we'll tell you. Nevada: we do not sell covered information as defined by Nevada law.

Children's privacy

The app and website are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child sent us some, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Reports, photos, and corrections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in a private, access-restricted, encrypted store only the publisher can review. No system is perfectly secure, so we collect as little as we can.

Region handling (EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

We are based in, and store data in, the United States. When your device region is set to the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland:

  • Photo of ingredients and Product corrections are unavailable. No label photos or corrections are captured, saved, or sent, and no photo, text, or barcode goes to us or to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts. Barcode lookups still work (see Third-party services).
  • Anonymous ingredient reporting is hidden, and no reports are sent.
  • If your region changes to one of these, the features turn off and pending uploads are canceled and never sent. Photos and unsent corrections already saved stay on your device to view or delete only (Settings → Label photos / My corrections).

We respect these regions' strong data-protection laws; because open-database contributions are public and permanent, we can't yet offer these features there in a way that fits those laws. Other features work normally. Region follows your device setting, which may not match where you are.

Changes to this policy

Each version has its own revision label and effective date, shown at the top. For material changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect and ask for consent again where the law or this policy requires (for example, before you keep using Photo of ingredients or Product corrections, or before we send wider ingredient reports). We won't use data we already have in a materially new way without your consent.
