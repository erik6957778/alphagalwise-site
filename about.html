About WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGIES LLC

WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGIES LLC is an Oklahoma limited liability company that builds and publishes Alpha-Gal Wise™ for iPhone.

Our founders

The company was founded by Erik and Susan, a husband and wife in rural Oklahoma. Susan lives with alpha-gal syndrome, a tick-related allergy to mammal-derived ingredients. Reading every label by hand for food, medicine, and household products takes time, so we built Alpha-Gal Wise™ to make it faster.

What we do

We make simple tools that help people with alpha-gal syndrome read ingredient labels. Our first product is Alpha-Gal Wise™, which lets you choose your own triggers and scan product barcodes to flag ingredients that might be a problem for you.
