Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback about Alpha-Gal Wise™? Email us at support@alphagalwise.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

What does Alpha-Gal Wise do?

You choose your triggers, like red meat, dairy, gelatin, or glycerin, then scan a product barcode. Alpha-Gal Wise™ shows ingredients that might be a problem for you and explains why.

Does a product with no flags mean I can use it?

No. A product with no flagged ingredients only means the app did not find a match in the data it has. Data can be incomplete or out of date, and manufacturers change formulas. Always check the actual label, and ask your allergist if you are unsure.

What kinds of products can I scan?

Food, medicine, and personal care and household products such as shampoo, lotion, and laundry detergent. Coverage depends on what is in the product databases we use.

Where does the product data come from?

Mostly from Open Food Facts, a free, community-built database (ODbL; images CC BY-SA), and its sister project Open Beauty Facts (ODbL; images CC BY-SA), and some drug label data from openFDA. openFDA does not endorse this app.

A product is missing or the ingredients look wrong. What should I do?

Rely on the physical label. You can also email us the barcode and product name at support@alphagalwise.com, or add or fix the product on Open Food Facts.

Is the app free?

Yes. It’s free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Your triggers and settings are kept on your device.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Email support@alphagalwise.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version.

Medical disclaimer

Not medical advice. Alpha-Gal Wise™ helps you read labels. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Product and ingredient data can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong, and formulas change. Always check the actual product label, and talk with your allergist or clinician about your own triggers.

If you think you are having an allergic reaction, call 911 or your local emergency number right away.
