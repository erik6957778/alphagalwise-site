The app is for general information only. It is not a substitute for a doctor, allergist, or pharmacist. A product with no flagged ingredients only means the app found no match in the data it has. It does not mean the product is free of your triggers or that it is right for you.

You are responsible for reading the actual label of every product before you eat, take, or use it, and for asking your doctor about your own diagnosis, triggers, and treatment.

If your device moves into one of these regions, label photos you already saved stay on your device for you to view or delete only.

T1. What it does. “Photo of ingredients” lets you photograph a food, cosmetic, or personal care product label. The app reads the text (OCR) and saves the photo, the text, the barcode (if any), and the date. You accept this section the first time you use the feature. If you choose “Not now,” you can still scan labels, but photos are not saved.
