Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Applies to: the Alpha-Gal Wise™ app and alphagalwise.com, operated by WILLIAMS TECHNOLOGIES LLC.
Version: Rev F. Effective date: November 4, 2026.

This policy covers “consumer health data” under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370, and Connecticut law, and supplements our Privacy Policy. Consumer health data means information linked or reasonably linkable to you that identifies your past, present, or future health status. Because simply using an alpha-gal app could suggest a health condition, we treat the data below as consumer health data.

The short version. Your triggers, scans, saved photos, and typed corrections stay on your device. We collect consumer health data only with your consent, share it only with service providers (plus Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts for photos you Share and corrections you Send), and never sell it.

Consumer health data we collect

  • Anonymous ingredient reports (only if you turn them on): flagged ingredients (unrecognized, or rated Avoid or Ambiguous) and the app's general rating for each, nearby label text and how the app sorted it, product barcode, public name, brand, and ingredient text, label source, app version, platform, date (no time of day), and a random per-report ID. Full field list.
  • Label photos you Share (each one you Share): the cropped photo without location or camera data, its scrubbed text, barcode, date, app version and platform, consent version, and a random withdrawal code.
  • Product corrections you Send (each one you Send): corrected name, brand, and ingredient text, barcode, category, any attached photo (cropped, without location or camera data), date, app version and platform, consent version, and a random withdrawal code.
  • Network information: the IP address our hosting provider logs when the app checks for settings at launch (kept up to 7 days).
  • Support emails: your email address and anything you tell us, which may include health information.

We do not collect your triggers, allergens, symptoms, reactions, diagnosis, scan history, unshared photos, unsent corrections, or location, and we don't use geofencing. Barcode lookups go directly from the app to Open Food Facts; we don't receive them or keep a list of what you scan.

Website analytics: alphagalwise.com uses GoatCounter only on the website, never in the Alpha-Gal Wise™ app. It collects no consumer health data, counts visits in aggregate, stores no IP addresses or identifiers, and doesn't record searches or which products or ingredients you view.

Sources of consumer health data

Only you: what you choose to send from the app, technical details the app adds (app version and platform), our hosting provider's security and delivery logs (including the launch settings-check IP), and emails you send us. We don't buy it or get it from other companies.

How we use consumer health data

  • To improve ingredient matching and fix wrong ratings or misread label text.
  • To review photos you Share and corrections you Send and, if they pass review, add them to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To keep our systems secure, prevent abuse, and comply with the law.

Never for sale, advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we don't try to re-identify anyone from de-identified data.

Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We share it only with service providers processing it for us under contract and on our instructions:

  • Supabase (U.S. database and storage hosting) and its network provider Cloudflare: reports, shared photos, corrections, and delivery logs.
  • ImprovMX (email forwarding) and Google Gmail (email hosting): messages to support@alphagalwise.com.

Third parties, only with your per-item consent: a photo you Share or correction you Send, with its text and barcode, goes to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, or Open Products Facts (all run by the same independent nonprofit in France) if it passes review, and becomes public. It carries only one fixed app-wide ID that cannot identify you. Ingredient reports are never sent to them automatically. We may also disclose data if the law requires it. Never to advertisers.

Consent and withdrawing consent

  • Ingredient reports: only after you choose “Send anonymous reports” or turn on Settings → Help improve ingredient matching. Reports on Avoid or Ambiguous ingredients need consent to the current version (version 2). Turn off anytime.
  • Label photos: collected only if you tap Share on that photo; that separate, per-photo consent also covers sharing it with the project. Withdraw unpublished photos in Settings → Label photos.
  • Product corrections: collected only if you tap Send. Withdraw unpublished ones in Settings → My corrections.

Saying no never limits other features. Withdrawal stops future collection and deletes unpublished items; it can't undo anything already published.

How long we keep it

Reports: de-identified, deleted or aggregated within 90 days. Shared photos and corrections: de-identified, deleted right after review or within 30 days of upload if not reviewed. IP logs: up to 7 days. Support emails: only as long as needed. Details: Privacy Policy.

We do not sell consumer health data

We do not sell consumer health data and will not without your signed, written authorization as the law requires. Submitting photos or corrections to the projects involves no payment or other value.

Your rights and how to exercise them

If you live in Washington, Nevada, or Connecticut (and some other states), you have the right to:

  • Access: confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy;
  • Know who receives it: get a list of third parties and service providers we share it with, and how to contact them;
  • Delete: have us and our service providers delete it;
  • Withdraw consent: stop our collection and sharing anytime.

In the app: turn off reports, or withdraw photos or corrections in Settings; deleting the app deletes on-device data. By email: support@alphagalwise.com, subject “Health Data Request,” with your state, your request, and anything that helps us find your data (e.g. date and product).

  • We respond within 45 days, and may extend once by 45 more days with notice and reason.
  • No charge, and no different treatment for asking.
  • We keep no names or device IDs with reports and photos, so we may not find data linked to you; if so, we'll say why. Anonymous reports can't be linked to anyone.
  • An authorized agent may request for you, as your state's law allows.

How to appeal a decision

If we decline your request in whole or part, reply to our decision or email support@alphagalwise.com with subject “Health Data Appeal” within a reasonable time. We'll respond in writing within 45 days, explaining what we did and why. If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state Attorney General:

  • Washington: Washington State Office of the Attorney General
  • Nevada: Nevada Attorney General
  • Connecticut: Connecticut Attorney General

Changes to this policy

Each version has its own revision label and effective date, shown at the top. We'll ask for your consent before collecting, using, or sharing consumer health data in a new way.
